# Playbook — Setting Up and Running the Channel

## Day 0: setup (about 1 hour)
1. **Pick one home platform.** Telegram channels and X are the fastest to
   operate with an agent; add others later as mirrors, not as separate jobs.
2. **Build your watchlist (30-50 sources).** Start with vendor blogs and
   official accounts in your niche. A source earns its place by breaking real
   news, not by posting often.
3. **Create three files** (templates included in this pack):
   - `candidate-pool` — raw finds, never auto-published.
   - `ledger` — the append-only record of everything published.
   - `watchlist` — your sources, reviewed weekly.
4. **Fix your slots.** Three publishing windows a day (e.g. 09:00, 13:00,
   20:30 in your audience's timezone). Put them in your calendar; the agent
   works to the slots, not to inspiration.
5. **Give the agent the skill.** Drop `SKILL.md` into your agent's skill
   folder (Claude: `~/.claude/skills/ai-news-operator/SKILL.md`, or paste it
   as project instructions for other agents) and tell it your channel name,
   platforms, slots and timezone.

## Daily rhythm (agent does this; you spot-check)
- **Morning slot:** collect overnight candidates -> verify top items ->
  draft -> publish 1-2 -> log.
- **Midday slot:** publish 1-2 held items; reply to substantive comments.
- **Evening slot:** publish final 1-2; park leftovers with notes.
- **End of day:** one-line report — posts published, sources that failed,
  anything awaiting your decision.

## Growth rules that actually worked
- **Consistency is the growth strategy.** 90 days of 3-5 verified posts beat
  any launch stunt.
- **Cross-promote inside your own matrix first** (profile links on every
  platform you run), then directories, then mutual promotion with channels
  your size — same tier, no repeat partner within 30 days.
- **One forwardable post a week:** a summary, a comparison table, or a
  "everything that changed this week" roundup. Design it to be forwarded.
- **Post-level SEO:** the exact model/product name in the first line, the
  words a user would search, and the source link in every post.

## Quality incidents and how to handle them
- **You posted something wrong:** correct it in a follow-up post the same
  day, note it in the ledger, and tighten the checklist where it failed.
  Never silently delete and pretend.
- **A source keeps failing verification:** demote it to "leads only" — it can
  point you to stories but can never be the cited source.
- **Platform send failures:** switch to the official API/bridge path; log
  the failure count. Three failures on one path = change the path, not the
  effort.

## Metrics that matter (weekly)
- Posts published vs planned (target: 100%).
- Verification rejects (healthy: some — zero rejects means the gate is off).
- Saves/forwards per post (the only honest growth signal early on).
- Subscriber delta. Slow is fine; negative after a quality incident is the
  alarm.
