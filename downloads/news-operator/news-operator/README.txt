# News Channel Operator — Claude Skill Pack

Run a daily AI news channel that never posts hallucinations.

This pack turns your AI assistant into a disciplined channel operator:
it collects stories from official sources only, runs every claim through a
two-source fact-check gate, drafts in a consistent practitioner voice,
publishes on fixed slots, and keeps an append-only ledger so no story ever
runs twice.

## What's inside
- `SKILL.md` — the operating rules your agent follows (drop it into your
  skills folder and it just works with Claude; paste it as instructions for
  other agents).
- `PLAYBOOK.md` — day-0 setup, the daily rhythm, growth rules, and how to
  handle quality incidents.
- `templates/` — the three working files: candidate pool, fact-check
  checklist, and the news ledger.

## Who this is for
Creators and solo operators running AI news on Telegram, X, Facebook,
Instagram, YouTube or Bilibili who want daily output without daily effort —
and without the credibility damage of posting AI-invented "news".

## Honest note
This pack will not make a channel grow by itself. It gives you the part that
is actually hard to improvise: a verification discipline and a publishing
system that survives contact with a real news cycle. Growth still comes from
showing up every day. This makes showing up cheap.
