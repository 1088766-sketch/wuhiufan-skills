What problem does it solve?

The agent interviews you in rounds — what each job was actually for, what changed because you were there, where the numbers are — and keeps a facts file as it goes. The resume is assembled from that file and nothing else: bullets follow verb, scope, result, and every claimed skill needs the story that proves it. Tailoring per application re-ranks the same facts for what each employer keeps repeating, with a version log. A final pass reads every bullet the way the interviewer who will challenge it would.

Who is it for?

Anyone whose career does not fit the blank boxes of a resume template — career changers and quiet achievers most of all.

Honest limitations

It will not invent employers, dates, metrics or skills, and it pushes back if you ask — a resume that survives your own interview is the whole point. It drafts and tailors; it does not submit applications or guarantee interviews. An original Wuhiufan Skills workflow, written from scratch; no third-party prompts or text are reproduced.

Frequently asked questions

I hate talking about myself. Will this still work?

That is who it is built for. The questions are concrete — what was broken when you arrived, what number is attached to your work — and the facts file grows from your answers, not from self-promotion.

Can it tailor to a specific job post?

Yes, that is stage three: the post's repeated words are matched honestly against your facts, the top bullets and summary are re-ranked, and the version is logged.
