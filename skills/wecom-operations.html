The problem it solves

WeCom keeps documents, calendar, to-dos and meetings in four separate corners, and none of them remind you about the others — so decisions get lost, promises made in chat never become tasks, and meetings arrive without their documents.

Who it is for

Anyone whose work runs inside WeCom (Enterprise WeChat) and who wants a daily operating rhythm instead of four disconnected apps.

What you get

  • SKILL.md with the morning brief: calendar with documents linked, to-dos by deadline, document deltas since yesterday.
  • A document order: one naming rule, one canonical home, a weekly index by project.
  • The meeting pipeline: agenda before, decisions and owned to-dos extracted the same day.
  • A ten-minute end-of-day sweep and a weekly review that names stale documents and heavy days ahead.

Honest limitations

It organises what your account can already see — no unlocking, scraping or permission bypass, and it is not an admin or monitoring tool. It assumes WeCom's standard features; heavily customised enterprise setups may need the steps adapted. Decisions stay yours.

FAQ

Does it access anything beyond my own WeCom account?

No. Every routine works inside what your account already sees. Anything your role cannot access stays invisible — that is stated as a boundary, not a bug.

What does a morning brief actually contain?

Today's meetings in order with their documents linked or flagged missing, to-dos split overdue / today / this week with a decision demanded on the overdue ones, and what changed in your documents since yesterday.

How much time does the system take to run?

The brief is a read, the sweep is ten minutes at day's end, and the review is a short Friday pass. The point is that everything else — hunting documents, remembering promises — stops taking time.

Will it write my documents or attend meetings?

No. It keeps order around the work: agendas ready, notes turned into owned to-dos, stale documents named. The work itself stays yours.
