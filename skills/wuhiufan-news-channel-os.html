What problem does it solve?

Most channels don't die from bad content. They die from drift: topics picked from memory, a fact nobody checked because "it was everywhere", the same story published twice because the first post wasn't written down anywhere, a "review" that never happens because the numbers live in five different apps. The operator knows all of this matters and still ends up running the channel out of a chat window and a notes file — because building the system feels like a project, and the channel needs a post today. So the system never gets built, and the drift compounds quietly until the channel is either sloppy or abandoned.

How does it work?

News Channel OS is the finished system, ready to duplicate: the structure distilled from a live, daily-operating news channel, generalized so it fits any beat, with every piece of real channel data removed. Five databases form one line. Topic Pool — every candidate lands here first, with its source link, source type, a 1–5 relevance score, a timeliness class, and a status; no link, no entry. Fact Check — the gate: each checked topic gets a row with its claim count, its two-source status, and the check notes; blocked claims get cut or the topic gets dropped, with the reason recorded. Publish Ledger — the single source of truth for what went out: platform, timestamp, sequential entry number, the verified live link, and performance filled at +24h. Schedule — the day as three passes (scan / gate-and-publish / wrap) plus a weekly retro session, each with a role and an action checklist. Retro Log — one entry a week: best content picked by engagement rate, attribution with honesty grades (Confirmed / Plausible / Guess), and exactly three next-week actions. A Home dashboard ties it together — pipeline board, gate queue, today's sessions, latest posts — and three guide pages carry the full SOP texts.

Honest limits

This is a template, not a service. Nobody runs your channel, writes your posts, or checks your facts for you — the OS gives the work a structure and a gate; the work is still yours. It contains no real channel data: the structure is distilled from a live operation, but every sample row is fictional, and no subscriber counts, revenue figures, or source lists from any real channel are included or promised. It is not an automation: it doesn't post, scrape, or verify anything by itself. The gate rules are deliberately strict — if your channel's model is speed-at-any-cost aggregation, this OS will feel like friction. That friction is the product.

Frequently asked questions

How do I receive the template?

Your purchase includes a Notion duplicate link (in the download). Open it, click Duplicate, and the whole workspace copies into your Notion account. A free Notion plan is enough.

I don't run a "news" channel. Does it still fit?

Yes, if you publish on a rhythm and facts matter to your readers: research briefings, deals channels, industry trackers, and community digests all run on the same line. If you publish fiction or pure opinion, the fact-check gate is more than you need.
