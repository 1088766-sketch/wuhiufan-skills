Honest limitations (please read)

No image model is included: this is a workflow and specification skill; it produces finished images only in runtimes with an image tool, elsewhere it delivers layout selections, complete design specs and ready-to-paste prompts. Text rendering is a process, not a promise: the inspection-and-repair loop catches and fixes garbled lettering, but no workflow can guarantee a model renders a long Chinese title perfectly on the first pass — which is why the skill defaults to textless bases plus typeset overlays whenever exact lettering matters. Book mode is visual translation, not reading: it works from documented content and labels interpretation as such; it does not summarize or replace the book, and it will not invent scenes, quotes or characters the book doesn't contain. Credits are factual or absent: titles and names are filled in exactly as published or as you supply them; the skill never fabricates awards, reviews, adaptations or endorsements.
