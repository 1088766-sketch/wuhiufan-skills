Honest limitations (please read)

Bring your own image generation: the art engine is whatever image capability your runtime provides; it is not included. The bundled scripts only assemble and pack — they run offline with Python 3 + Pillow, but they do not draw the character. Consistency is a discipline, not a guarantee: the anchor-card method reduces drift sharply and the QA gate catches what slips through, but no prompt method can promise zero drift on every frame from every image model; expect to regenerate individual stickers. Platform rules win: sticker size, dimensions and format limits differ per chat platform and change over time — checking your target platform's current spec is your step. Your likeness, your responsibility: the workflow confirms the photo is yours, your pet, or a character you may use; it will not build a pack from someone else's likeness.

Frequently asked questions

Does it generate the drawings itself?

No. Generation uses your runtime's image capability; the package provides the identity-lock workflow and the offline assembly/packing scripts.

How many stickers in a pack?

The default plan is 16 frames/stickers per pack, adjustable in the beat sheet.

What do I need to run the scripts?

Python 3 and Pillow. Everything else is plain Markdown in any SKILL.md runtime.
