What problem does it solve?

Stage one fills a research card per candidate: the demand signal and its source, the competitor set, landed-cost math with every fee named — and the complaint that repeats in competitors' 1-star reviews, which becomes your product brief. Stage two is a compliance gate before any listing work: IP, claims you can actually evidence, restricted-category requirements, each checked and dated on the card. Then listing production in the buyer's language — localized, not just translated — a launch checklist instead of a mood, and a weekly review that records kill decisions with their reasons so dead products stay dead.

The loop is platform-neutral — the card, gate and checklist apply to Amazon, TikTok Shop or your own store. Platform-specific rules are verified at the gate, with the check date written on the card.
