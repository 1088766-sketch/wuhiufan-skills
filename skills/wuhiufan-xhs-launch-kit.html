Honest limitations (please read)

Advisor, not operator: the kit never auto-publishes, scrapes at scale, buys engagement, or builds fake personas — and it will decline those requests. Publishing remains your step; Xiaohongshu offers no official third-party posting API. Data modules have two gears: Hotspot and Account Audit reach full quantitative power with a third-party Xiaohongshu data API key (not included); without one they run on public search and screenshots and label their conclusions accordingly — they never fabricate engagement numbers. Visual modules need an image tool: Cover and Infographic produce bitmaps in runtimes with built-in image generation; elsewhere they deliver complete plans and prompts. No guaranteed outcomes: the kit raises the odds and removes unforced errors; it does not — and honestly cannot — promise follower counts, viral notes, or revenue. English edition: this package is written in English; the workflows and rubrics are language-agnostic, and your agent can work with you in Chinese while using it.
