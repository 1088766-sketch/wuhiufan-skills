Honest limits (read before buying)

The agent does the thinking; rendering stages need Node.js 22+ and a headless Chrome/Chromium on your machine. Illustrations and illustration-band covers need an image channel you provide (a logged-in image-capable CLI, or your own OpenAI/Gemini key); every text format works without one. The finest local narration voices and built-in speech recognition are Apple Silicon territory; on other machines narration falls back to free online neural TTS, and a caption-less video needs a subtitle file or a recognizer you supply. This is a workflow and quality-discipline pack: it does not include a hosted service, and it will not post anything for you.

Frequently asked questions

Is this just prompts?

No — it is a staged pipeline with file contracts, a data schema, mechanical acceptance checks, and a publishing/results loop. The discipline (verbatim ledger, number tracing, small-sample honesty) is the product.

Can it keep my article word-for-word?

Yes. Verbatim mode typesets only, then reads the output back block by block against a ledger of the source.

Chinese or English sources?

Both. Rewrites default to the audience's language; full translations keep block-for-block structure and are checked for omissions and lost numbers.

What does it cost to run?

The text pipeline: nothing beyond your agent subscription. Optional image generation uses your own channel and its pricing.
