Frequently asked questions

Does it include voice or video assets?

No. No TTS service, no editing software, no media files — the package is the workflow, the visual specification and two utility scripts.

How is accuracy handled?

Every claim passes a research gate before scripting and is classified verified, soften or dropped; unsourced claims never enter the script.

What do the scripts need?

Python 3; qc_check.py uses ffprobe/ffmpeg when present and otherwise prints a manual checklist instead of failing.
