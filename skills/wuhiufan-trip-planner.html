What problem does it solve?

It keeps one living itinerary where every entry is labeled Booked, Chosen or Idea — so budgets stop being fictional and nothing gets double-booked — and it runs a feasibility pass over the whole plan: connections against minimum times with bad luck priced in, last-mile realism (arrival + immigration + bags + transfer is the time you actually reach the stay), closing days and last-entry times from the venue, check-in windows against midnight arrivals, and one named buffer per travel day.

Who is it for?

Travelers who plan in documents and spreadsheets and want the plan checked, not just listed — couples, families, and anyone stitching flights, stays and ground transport into one trip.

What do you get?

  • The workflow as a SKILL.md: constraints first, anchors before details, geography-respecting day plans, a dated money view, travel-day sheets
  • A complete itinerary template (constraints, anchors, day plans, money view, travel-day sheets)
  • The feasibility checklist, item by item

Honest limitations

This workflow plans and checks — it does not book, and it tells you to re-verify prices and availability at booking time; every price in the plan carries the date it was seen. It catches arithmetic and geography, not weather, strikes or luck. Buffers are its honest answer to those. Frequently asked questions

Does it book flights or hotels for me?

No. It produces a checked plan with anchors, prices (dated) and booking order. Booking stays with you, on purpose — the plan's job is to make sure what you book actually fits together.

What does the feasibility pass actually check?

Minimum connection times plus a class of bad luck, secure-area resets on airport changes, realistic arrival-to-door times, check-in/out windows, venue closing days and last entry, timed-ticket anchoring, human limits (first day after long-haul, walking loads), and one buffer per travel day.

What exactly do I receive?

A ZIP with the SKILL.md workflow, the itinerary template, the feasibility checklist, an install guide, license and changelog.
