What problem does it solve?

Most episode workflows start at the script. This one starts at the brief: a one-sentence promise to the listener, or the topic is two episodes. Then a research pack where every claim that will be spoken as fact carries a source, a script written for ears (breath-length lines, signposts, a cold open, a closing line a listener can repeat at dinner), recording in scene-sized chunks so fixes are cheap, a three-pass QC listen (normal speed, 1.5x, laptop speakers), and a publish pack whose source list is the episode's receipt.

Who is it for?

Solo briefing shows, two-host conversations, interviews and narrated pieces — anyone whose agent can draft and organize but needs a production discipline around it.

What do you get?

  • The six-stage workflow as a SKILL.md your agent follows
  • An episode brief template and a script template with pronunciation notes
  • A voice direction and QC reference: casting, retake triggers, loudness housekeeping

Honest limitations

This is the directing workflow, not voices, recording tools or hosting — it runs whatever tools you already use. And it will not make an unsourced topic safe to publish: no research pack, no episode. An original workflow written from the author's own production practice; no third-party text is reproduced.

Frequently asked questions

Can I use it with synthetic voices?

Yes. The voice stage covers casting synthetic voices by role and freezing the choice in a show bible, plus chunk-based generation so a fix costs one chunk, not the episode.

My episodes are interviews. Does the script stage still apply?

The brief, research pack and publish pack apply unchanged. The script stage becomes a question route built from the research pack, with the same cold-open and closing-line discipline.

What exactly do I receive?

A ZIP with the SKILL.md workflow, brief and script templates, the voice and QC reference, an install guide, license and changelog.
