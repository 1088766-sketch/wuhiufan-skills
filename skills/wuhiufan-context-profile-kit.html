What problem does it solve?

One profile, five layers: an identity card of ten lines at most, standing preferences (formats, what to decide without asking, what never to do without asking), active projects with their current state, the people and accounts that recur in your work, and samples of your own voice. You build it by interview rather than by form, because the follow-ups are where the useful detail lives. The kit's real value is its maintenance rules — one source of truth, update on change not on schedule, facts dated, and never secrets, because the profile is meant to be pasted into tools and must be safe to paste.

Who is it for?

Anyone who works across several AI tools or agents — Claude, ChatGPT, Codex, Cursor, local agents — and is tired of starting from zero in each.

Honest limitations

A profile is only as current as its last edit; the rules reduce staleness, they do not abolish it. Some tools truncate long context — which is exactly why the identity card is first and short. An original Wuhiufan Skills product, written from scratch; no third-party prompts or text are reproduced.

Frequently asked questions

How is this different from a tool's built-in memory?

Built-in memory belongs to that tool and cannot move. The profile is a file you own: paste it into any tool, keep it in a repo, correct it once and every tool benefits.

Is it safe to paste into tools?

It is designed to be: the rules ban passwords, keys, codes and ID numbers from the profile. It records where things are kept, never the things themselves.
