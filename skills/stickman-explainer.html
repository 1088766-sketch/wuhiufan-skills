Stickman Explainer Video

Stickman Explainer Video is a skill that turns a one-line topic into a finished 30-second stickman explainer video: script, storyboard, code-rendered scenes, narration timing and subtitles, exported as a ready-to-post MP4.

For educators and creators making short explainer videosClaude CodeCodexCursor

$1.99 — in production, joining the library soon

What is inside the package?

  • SKILL.md with scripting and storyboard rules
  • Rendering scripts for scenes, narration sync and subtitles
  • Timing rules that keep voice and picture locked
  • Worked example: the sample video and its project files

How do I install it?

Download the ZIP, unzip it into your agent's skills directory (for Claude Code: ~/.claude/skills/), restart the agent, and ask for the output in plain language. The included install guide covers Claude Code, Codex and Cursor step by step.

Frequently asked questions

What is the current status of this skill?

In production. A sample video on the topic of forward deployed engineering has been rendered end to end with this workflow.

Do I need video editing software?

No. The pipeline renders with code and free tools; the install guide lists exactly what to set up.

I can't draw at all. Is that a problem?

No. Drawing is the agent's job — the skill renders the stickman scenes itself from your topic. Your part is the topic and a read-through of the storyboard before it renders.

How long can the videos be?

The format is tuned for short explainers — a single idea in a couple of minutes or less. Longer topics are split into a series rather than stretched, and the narration timing rules keep picture and voice in sync across parts.
