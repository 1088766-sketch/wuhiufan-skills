What problem does it solve?

Decks fail at the start. Gate one is a brief interview: who is in the room, what they should approve when the last slide lands, how many minutes you really have, what must not appear. Gate two is an outline where every slide title is a claim, not a topic, with its evidence named — approved by you before anything is built. Then the build goes slide by slide: the claim, the proof that earns it, speaker notes that are not the slide read aloud. Slides without evidence are marked [needs evidence] instead of decorated around, and a final consistency pass makes the numbers agree across the deck and the titles alone tell the story.

Who is it for?

Anyone who presents to a specific room — founders pitching, operators reporting up, speakers with ten real minutes and a decision to win.

Honest limitations

It structures and checks; it does not supply design talent or your missing data, and it will not fabricate results to fill a proof slide. A deck that lies prettier is still a deck that lies. An original Wuhiufan Skills workflow, written from scratch; no third-party prompts or text are reproduced.
