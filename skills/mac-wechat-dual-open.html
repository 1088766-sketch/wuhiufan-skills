FAQ

How does it work, in one sentence?

The clone is a copy of WeChat with a different bundle identifier, so macOS gives it its own data folder and its own login — two apps, two accounts, one machine.

Is my original WeChat touched?

Never. The original stays exactly as installed; the clone lives separately, and deleting the clone undoes everything.

What happens when WeChat updates?

Update the original normally, then re-clone from it — about five minutes. Updating the clone in place is the fragile path, and the skill routes around it.

Does it spoof my device or identity?

No. Each instance is an ordinary login of your own real account. The clone changes the app's identity on your Mac, not how the account behaves.
