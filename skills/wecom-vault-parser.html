The problem it solves

Decisions, files and approvals pile up inside WeCom chats, and once the app scrolls past them they are effectively gone — unsearchable at exactly the moment you need to prove what was agreed.

Who it is for

Professionals whose work record lives in WeCom (Enterprise WeChat) and who need their own records backed up, searchable, and ready for an orderly handover.

What you get

  • SKILL.md with the parse: snapshot the local store, open the copy with a locally read key, parse per conversation.
  • Markdown per conversation plus a CSV master index — the file you actually search day to day.
  • An export log with counts in and out, placeholders for server-only attachments, and a confidentiality note.
  • Verification against the app on the three most recent conversations.

Honest limitations

Mac only, for the account signed in on that machine. The local store holds only what this machine synced — older history that never reached the Mac cannot appear. Attachments never downloaded locally export as placeholders with filename and date.

FAQ

Does it read anything from WeCom's servers?

No. It parses the local store on your Mac, from a copy — the live store is never modified and nothing is requested remotely.

My local history seems incomplete. Why?

The parser can only render what this machine synced. History that lives only on your phone or the server is not in the local store, and the skill says so instead of inventing it.

Is this a monitoring tool for my team?

No. It parses your own signed-in account's records for your archive and handover. It does not watch, scrape or report on anyone else.

What do I get at the end?

Per-conversation Markdown, a CSV index you can open in any spreadsheet, locally available attachments linked, and an export log reconciling counts.
