Honest limitations (please read)

Paper only: this package produces briefs, scripts and storyboards; it contains no filming, video-generation, voice or editing tools, and it does not produce storyboard images unless your runtime has an image tool and you ask for them. Executable depends on your tools and crew: the storyboard is written to be shot with the resources recorded in the brief; if your units, cast, app build or post capacity differ, the board must be revised to match. The example product is fictional: HydraLoop was invented for this package; its brief, figures and test results are illustrative planning documents, not claims about any real product. Claims are your responsibility: the workflow enforces claim discipline and flags risky lines, but the facts about your product come from you, and their accuracy stays with you.
