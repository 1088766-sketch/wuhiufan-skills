Frequently asked questions

What is the current status of this skill?

In production. The workflow behind it publishes multiple episodes per week on a running children's English channel.

What age range is it tuned for?

The reference pipeline is tuned for ages 4 to 9, with short sentences, clear narration and bright scenes.

I want to post stories regularly. What does one episode actually involve?

The slow part is setup: locking the characters and the narration voice once. After that, an episode is a production run — story in, narration first, pictures and sound layered to the voice — not a redesign. The pipeline is built for a repeatable cadence, which is how the author's own channel ships daily.

Do I bring my own story, or does it write one?

Either. You can hand it a finished story text, or a one-line idea and let the workflow draft the episode script in the house format — short lines, one idea per shot, written for a 4-to-9-year-old listener.
