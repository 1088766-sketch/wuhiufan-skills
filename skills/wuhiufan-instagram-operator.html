What is inside the package?

Thirteen specialist skills (plain Markdown, standard SKILL.md format), five small Python tools that run locally (standard library only, no network, no API key), a 20-formula hook library, a 100-point profile rubric, and a voice-profile template:

  • Reel Script Desk — one idea into a shootable Reel: three hooks from the formula library, each scored, then script, on-screen text and a timed beat sheet
  • Niche Outlier Radar — what is working in your niche, ranked by outlier multiple over each account's own median; builds your swipe file
  • Caption Lint — the caption, the feed's 125-character window printed as a box, and checks for length, the five-hashtag cap, search terms and exactly one ask
  • Carousel Smith — a cover that earns the swipe, one idea per slide, tension and payoff in turns, one ask at the end
  • Story Funnel — the daily story arc: which sticker does which job, and how a sequence warms up a conversation the viewer starts
  • Profile Scorecard — your profile against 12 checks and 100 points, rewrites ordered by points lost
  • Week Planner — the week on one page: slots with format, formula and evidence, the ten accounts worth engaging with, production batches
  • Plain Voice Pass — strips invisible copy-paste characters, normalises typography, flags stock phrasing, scores the draft before and after
  • Comment Craft — comments on other people's posts, matched to what the post actually is; specific or silent, never flame-emoji filler
  • Reply Triage — the thread under your own post sorted into keyword, lead, question, substance, support and noise, drafted in that order
  • DM Playbook — keyword delivery, the story-reply answer, the collaboration pitch, and at most two follow-ups
  • Repurpose Week — one video, podcast or newsletter into a week of posts that each stand alone
  • Post Audit — published posts ranked by outlier multiple and sends per reach, ending in four instructions for next week

Who is it for?

Creators and small teams who post on Instagram, or are about to, and want a repeatable desk system instead of daily improvisation. It fits best if you make short-form video and write your own captions — the pack's whole point is that the words have to survive being said out loud.

Honest limitations (please read)

These skills write; you post. Nothing here publishes, comments, follows or sends DMs by itself — automated posting and engagement violate Instagram's terms and get accounts restricted, so every skill ends with a copy-ready draft and your approval. That is the design, not a missing feature. No paid dependencies: no API key, no scraper subscription, no third-party service; the research desk reads at human speed with you driving your own browser. No invented numbers: if a draft needs a metric you have not supplied, it comes back with a placeholder and a flag — a hook scorer can kill a weak hook before you shoot it, but it cannot tell you which of two decent hooks will travel. Platform numbers go stale: the hashtag cap moved from 30 to 5 in December 2025, and if this pack and Instagram disagree when you read it, Instagram is right.
