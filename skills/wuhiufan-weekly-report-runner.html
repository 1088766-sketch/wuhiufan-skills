What problem does it solve?

Rescue mode reconstructs the week from where it left traces — calendar, sent work, task lists — plus your five-minute brain dump, and sorts it into done, in progress, blocked and dropped. Dropped is a real category: quietly vanished work is how next week's surprises are born. The report answers the reader's three questions in order (what changed, why it matters, what do you need from me), and next week's actions come out as a contract with an owner, a finish line and a date. Routine mode then writes the runbook — gather order, skeleton, reader preferences — and installs the two-minute daily habit that makes the next report a assembly job, not an excavation.

Who is it for?

Team leads, operators, freelancers reporting to clients — anyone whose Friday disappears into status updates.

Honest limitations

It cannot report work it cannot see; unrecorded work enters only through your brain dump, and the runbook says so plainly. It writes the report — it does not send it or chase the actions. An original Wuhiufan Skills workflow, written from scratch; no third-party prompts or text are reproduced.
